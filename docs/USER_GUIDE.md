# Storm & Johnson — User Guide

A simple guide to using the Storm & Johnson website.

---

## What is this website?

Storm & Johnson Limited sells premium energy-efficient lighting in Nigeria — solar street lights, LED bulbs, panel lights, and more. Brands include **Pololux**, **Qiming**, and **Liton**.

You can browse products, send enquiries, apply to become a distributor, or contact the team. Staff use the **Admin** area to manage the catalogue and read incoming messages.

---

## For visitors

### Browse products

1. Open **Products** from the menu (or **Order a Product** on the home page).
2. Use **Search**, **Brand**, **Category**, and **Sort** to find what you need.
3. Click a product to see photos, price, specs, and full details.

### Order or ask about a product

On any product page:

- Fill in the **enquiry form** (name, email, phone, quantity, delivery address) and submit — the team will follow up.
- Or tap **WhatsApp** to chat directly.

### Become a distributor

Go to **Become a Distributor**, fill in your business details, and submit. The team will review your application.

### General contact

Go to **Contact Us** to send a message or see phone, email, and address.

### WhatsApp

The green **WhatsApp** button on every page opens a chat with Storm & Johnson.

### About the company

**About Us** explains who Storm & Johnson are, who they serve, and answers common questions (delivery, bulk orders, brands, etc.).

---

## For administrators

**Login:** Menu → **Admin** (or `/admin/login`)

After signing in you can:

| Section | What you do |
|---------|-------------|
| **Overview** | Read product enquiries, distributor applications, and contact messages |
| **Products** | Add, edit, or remove products (with photos, price, brand, category) |
| **Brands** | Manage brand names used on products |
| **Categories** | Manage product categories used for filtering |
| **Company** | Update contact details and social links shown on the site |

**Typical workflow for a new product:**

1. Add a **Brand** and **Category** (if they don’t exist yet).
2. Go to **Products** → **Add Product** → fill in details and images → save.
3. Check **Overview** for new customer enquiries.

**Logout** when you’re finished.
